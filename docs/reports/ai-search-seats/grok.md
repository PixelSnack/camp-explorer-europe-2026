## 2. What gets cited (measured studies 2025–2026 vs opinion)

Measured: Profound (30 million citations, Aug 2024–Jun 2025): ChatGPT top source Wikipedia 47.9%; Google AI Overviews Reddit 21%; Perplexity Reddit 46.7%. Confirmed via Barry post and SERC coverage.[[20]](https://www.seroundtable.com/chatgpt-google-aio-sources-39578.html)

GEO (Aggarwal et al., arXiv 2023/KDD 2024; 10,000 queries): adding quotations, statistics, and source citations lifted visibility 28–41% (Position-Adjusted Word Count); keyword stuffing hurt. Smaller/lower-ranked sites gained most relatively. Confirmed.[[21]](https://aisearch.global/insights/princeton-geo-paper-explained)

Cyrus Shepard compilation (7 May 2026; 54 experiments/patents/case studies over 2 years): 22–23 factors scored by repeatability/evidence strength. Top: URL accessibility (9.5), search rank (9.4), fan-out rank, preview controls, query-answer match. Domain authority weaker/mixed. LLMs.txt lowest. Correlation, not causation. Confirmed via his Substack.[[22]](https://signal.zyppy.com/p/ai-citation-ranking-factors)

Ahrefs schema (May 2026; 1,885 pages added JSON-LD Aug 2025–Mar 2026 vs 4,000 controls): no major citation uplift on Google AI Overviews (small –4.6% decline vs controls), AI Mode, or ChatGPT. Pages already cited were ~3x more likely to have schema (correlation). Confirmed.[[23]](https://ahrefs.com/blog/schema-ai-citations/)

Ahrefs CTR (updated Feb 2026; ~300k keywords, GSC Dec 2023 vs Dec 2025): AI Overviews correlated with 58% lower pos-1 CTR (from ~7.3% to 1.6% on AIO keywords). Earlier Apr 2025 version was 34.5%. Pew (22 Jul 2025; 900 US adults, ~68k searches Mar/Apr 2025): traditional result click 8% with AIO vs 15% without; AIO-internal link click 1%; session-end 26% vs 16%. Lily Ray posted the 1% figure. Confirmed.[[24]](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/)

Kevin Indig usability (2025–2026; 37 participants/7 tasks for AI Mode: 77.6% sessions zero external visits; 56 people/221 ChatGPT product-comparison tasks: 92.8% no meaningful open-web click). Featured NYT; Google called sample/tasks limited. Directional. Confirmed.[[25]](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/what-our-ai-mode-user-behavior-study-reveals-about-the-future-of-search/557697/?user_id=+&_hsenc=p2ANqtz--Rz_s77Jnk_mlXH7ZeWmz7tYGjbPpuwUnPnLDFs2KPh-Phjf_10ZmcowOwtjgS-qMRb7528U7uIwKvMZGspWy5-wJ4QGItbqYTxL5ltufR8YuLoiM&_hsmi=384054503)

Aleyda checklist (Jun 2025, opinion+practice): chunk-level retrieval, answer synthesis, citation-worthiness, topical breadth/depth, authoritativeness, crawlability. No sample size. 

Content shape that appears: lists/tables/FAQs/definitions/stats-with-sources/dates/author/provenance (GEO + practitioners). Headings/chunkable sections. Freshness mixed (some studies). Reddit/forums/YouTube/Wikipedia high in some engines (Profound). Brand mentions/DA mixed (Cyrus). Schema/structure correlation but adding it did not cause citations (Ahrefs). Rand Fishkin (Aug 2026): citation-tracking studies often misleading because models decide brands first. Opinion/caution.

## 3. Emerging standards and real adoption

llms.txt / llms-full.txt: proposed markdown map at root for LLMs (like robots.txt). Exists as file format. Google Search Central added one (Dec 2025, Barry); John Mueller comments via Lily Ray. Google docs: does not help or hurt Search rankings (updated Jun 2026). Ahrefs (Jun 2026; 137k sites): 28% of sample published one; 97% of those files received zero requests in May 2026; AI bots ~19.5% of the tiny traffic that existed; bots never probe for missing files. Cats.txt experiment (Aug 2026, Aleyda/Glenn): indexing ≠ use; "GEO astrology." Marie Haynes: only if agents need specific tools/MCP. Adoption of *reading* is near-zero per measured logs. Vaporware for citation/search as of mid-2026. Confirmed.[[26]](https://www.reddit.com/r/aeo/comments/1u6irf1/we_analyzed_137k_sites_97_of_llmstxt_files_never/)

Google-Extended: robots.txt *token* (not a crawler/UA) since 2023. Controls Gemini training/grounding use of already-crawled Googlebot content. Does *not* affect Search, AI Overviews, or AI Mode (those use Googlebot). Site allowing it (as of 13 Sep 2026) has no Search/AIO impact. Barry 2025: for Vertex, not AIO. Confirmed via docs/posts.[[27]](https://deepsmith.ai/blog/how-google-ai-overviews-reads-your-site)

Cloudflare pay-per-crawl: live feature (AI Crawl Control); charge per successful crawl (HTTP 200) via headers/402. Site owners set price; Cloudflare merchant. 2026 shift announced toward "pay-per-use" (value/citation vs mere crawl; experiments with Ceramic/You.com). Glenn Gabe Aug 2026. Site is Vercel, so inapplicable unless migrated. Real, evolving. Confirmed.[[28]](https://blog.cloudflare.com/making-ai-search-smarter/)

Agent-ready web: schema.org Actions / structured booking data: proposed/exists in schema but no 2025–2026 measured citation/agent-use studies found here. MCP servers, WebMCP, NLWeb, OKF (Google Cloud Open Knowledge Format, Jun 2026 news), IETF AI-preferences: proposed/early. Marie Haynes built an MCP example. Cloudflare AI crawl controls + Content Signals: real for CF users. Agentic commerce: OpenAI shopping research + checkout/agent mode live (2025 posts); Google agent payments: NOT FOUND in these X/web results. Mixed: some live (OpenAI agents), most proposed/vapor for typical sites.

## 4. Agents acting for users

ChatGPT agent mode (preview Plus/Pro/Business ~Oct 2025; shopping research Nov 2025): with permission, browses (search/scroll/click like a user), books appointments, makes purchases. OpenAI posts describe it directly. Shopping: clarifying questions, personalized guides, lookalikes, feedback loops. Confirmed.[[29]](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/1993018359102029889)

Gemini agents in Chrome / Perplexity Comet / Claude in Chrome / Operator-class: limited mentions (Marie Haynes on Gemini models in AI Mode; Glenn on Meta Muse example of calendar/booking). Specific summer-camp booking demos or vendor guidance for camp directories: NOT FOUND. Claude system-prompt leak analysis (Aleyda May 2025): searches/tools only when model knowledge insufficient; prefers compact/quotable structured sources.

Page properties that make a site usable (inferred from vendor descriptions + practitioner notes, not a single 2026 vendor "agent-ready" spec): clean/static HTML (many fetchers lack JS; site homepage is SPA so non-renderers see only title/meta/JSON-LD), stable selectors, accessible forms, structured data (events/offers), prices/availability/dates in visible text (not JS-only), no interstitials/paywalls that block agents, direct booking links. OpenAI: agent "searching, scrolling, and clicking" like a user. No published vendor checklist found beyond that. Ahrefs schema study implies visible HTML > hidden JSON-LD for retrieval.
