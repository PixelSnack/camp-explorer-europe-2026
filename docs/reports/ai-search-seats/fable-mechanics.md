Google, Bing and Apple render. OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity publish nothing on rendering. A non-rendering fetcher sees the site's title, description and JSON-LD and no camp names. The static pages are the fix.

Vendors' explicit statements: Google: "You don't need to create new machine readable files, AI text files, or markup" (ai-features). OpenAI: an allowed OAI-SearchBot plus allowed IPs is the whole inclusion requirement; llms.txt is mentioned nowhere in its publisher docs. Google Search Central blog (21 May 2025): unique non-commodity content, page experience, Googlebot not blocked, preview controls, structured data matching visible content, "clicks are higher quality" from AI Overview pages.

Infrastructure: Cloudflare sorts AI traffic into Search, Agent and Training; from 15 Sept 2026 blocks Agent and Training by default on ad-bearing pages for new zones. Content Signals in robots.txt. Pay-per-crawl closed beta (HTTP 402, crawler-price header). Vercel's AI-bots managed ruleset is off by default and does not distinguish training from search bots; Vercel verifies Web Bot Auth and lists chatgpt-operator, claude-user, claude-searchbot, google-agent, gemini-deep-research, perplexity-user as verified bots. Keep the ruleset off.

## 5. Measurement

ChatGPT appends utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral URLs (Publishers FAQ, Aug 2026); no utm_medium. Google: AI Overviews and AI Mode clicks are counted inside the Search Console "Web" search type; the separate "Search generative AI performance" report (worldwide since 31 Aug 2026) shows impressions only, by page, country, device and date; two results from one site count as one impression (answer/16984139; blog 3 June 2026). GA4 cannot separate AI Mode from organic: Google's GA4 "AI Assistant" channel definition "excludes Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode" (support.google.com/analytics/answer/9756891); that channel matches medium exactly "ai-assistant"; whether utm_source=chatgpt.com with no medium maps into it is not stated. Bing AI Performance (10 Feb 2026, expanded 16 June 2026 with Intents, Topics, Citation Share, Compare): citation counts, cited pages, grounding queries; sampled, daily, "does not represent traffic, clicks, or user engagement". No first-party citation reporting for Claude, Perplexity or the Gemini app. Cloudflare crawl-to-refer ratios (Anthropic 70,900:1 in June 2025 from the blog; the 2026 Radar figures are third-party write-ups).

## 6. Risks and documented mitigations

Summarised without a click: Google says AI Overview clicks are "higher quality"; Cloudflare's measurement says "very few users actually click through". max-snippet and data-nosnippet limit quoting but "more restrictive permissions will limit how your content is featured". Keep the thing the parent needs next one click deep. Misquoted: EBU and BBC study (Oct 2025; 22 broadcasters, 18 countries, 14 languages, 3,000+ responses): 45 percent of answers with at least one significant issue, 31 percent sourcing problems, Gemini worst at 76 percent. Dated per-fact statements with the check date make wrong quotes traceable. Training: disallowing Google-Extended also removes grounding in Gemini Apps and Vertex, so allowing it was right for citations. Scrapers: Cloudflare documented Perplexity rotating to a spoofed Chrome UA (4 Aug 2025); verify by published IP JSON, reverse DNS, or Web Bot Auth; Vercel's verified-bots directory already does this.

## 7. For this site

Ten actions: 1 ship the static pages with names, prices, dates, sources in initial HTML; 2 leave the rendered homepage alone, link new pages from it in plain anchors; 3 verify in Bing Webmaster Tools and read AI Performance weekly; 4 IndexNow for every new or changed static page; 5 the Search Console generative AI report into the weekly watch, control on include; 6 check date and source beside every figure, operator URL beside the price; 7 first paragraph answers the page's question in one or two self-contained sentences; 8 GA4 segment on utm_source=chatgpt.com, the AI Assistant channel and the referrers; 9 Content Signals line in robots.txt (harmless where unread); 10 keep Vercel's AI-bots ruleset off.

Five wastes: llms.txt for citations; special AI schema; blocking AI bots to force clicks; chasing a referrer for AI Mode; paying for a citation tracker before the pages exist.

Least sure: that OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User never execute JavaScript; that GA4's AI Assistant channel captures source-only chatgpt.com tags; the 2026 Cloudflare crawl-to-refer ratios.

Unreachable: OpenAI "ChatGPT Atlas for publishers" (404); no Claude-in-Chrome user agent in Anthropic docs.
