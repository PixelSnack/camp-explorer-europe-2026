## 2. What measured research actually supports

| Study/date | Sample and method | Finding and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggarwal et al., GEO**, [16 November 2023 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), subsequently KDD 2024 | GEO-bench; controlled rewriting of material supplied to a generative engine | Sources, statistics and quotations improved experimental visibility, sometimes substantially. This is foundational—not evidence of 2026 organic discovery or booking uplift. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735?utm_source=openai)) |
| **Ahrefs, AI Overview Brand Visibility**, [26 May 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/) | 75,000 brands; millions of AI Overview responses; Spearman correlations | Web mentions correlated with visibility at **0.664**, backlinks at **0.218**. Brand mentions are not page citations; correlation does not establish causation. ([ahrefs.com](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overview-brand-correlation/?utm_source=openai)) |
| **Semrush, Most-Cited Domains**, [10 November 2025](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai/) | 230,000 prompts, three platforms, 13 weekly snapshots; over 100 million citations | Reddit and Wikipedia were prominent, but citation patterns changed sharply. AI Mode favored Google-owned properties. This supports monitoring forums/video—not assuming permanent Reddit or YouTube preference. ([semrush.com](https://www.semrush.com/blog/most-cited-domains-ai/)) |
| **SE Ranking, llms.txt**, [7 November 2025](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/) | Approximately 300,000 domains; Spearman, XGBoost and SHAP analysis | No meaningful citation advantage associated with file presence. Observational evidence, not a randomized intervention or proof nobody reads the file. ([seranking.com](https://seranking.com/blog/llms-txt/)) |
| **Semrush/Kevin Indig, Ghost Citations**, [9 June 2026](https://www.semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/) | 3,981 domain appearances; 115 prompts, 14 countries, four systems | **62% of citations lacked a corresponding brand mention.** Being sourced does not necessarily build recognizable brand awareness. ([semrush.com](https://www.semrush.com/blog/the-ghost-citations-study/)) |
| **Critical Survey of GEO**, [July 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035) | Structured review of 45 studies | Discovery, retrieval, citation, factual contribution and user behavior require separate evaluation. Existing benchmarks do not establish durable commercial effects. ([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14035)) |

**Freshness:** Ahrefs analyzed **16.975 million cited URLs**, comparing page age/update measures across assistants and organic results; AI-cited pages were younger on average. This supports genuine maintenance, not merely changing dates. Source: [2025 study](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/); exact publication day **NOT FOUND**. ([ahrefs.com](https://ahrefs.com/blog/do-ai-assistants-prefer-to-cite-fresh-content/))

**Content shape:** headings, tables, FAQs and sourced evidence are explicitly recommended by Bing. Google rejects mandatory “chunking,” special AI schema and prescribed AI-only writing. Independent 2026 causal evidence that a specific FAQ, definition, author-box or schema format increases citations across systems: **NOT FOUND**. These are clarity/provenance practices, not established ranking switches. Sources: [Bing, 10 February 2026](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/February-2026/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview); [Google, 10 July 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). ## 3. Standards: actual implementation versus promised benefits

- **llms.txt/llms-full.txt:** the [proposal](https://llmstxt.org/) reached v2 on **10 August 2026**. Google Search explicitly ignores these files for visibility/ranking. Conversely, Chrome Lighthouse has an actual [llms.txt audit](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/agentic-browsing/llms-txt), n.d.—evidence of tooling adoption, not search ranking adoption. Automatic llms-full.txt consumption by the named search products: **NOT FOUND**. ([llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org/))
- **Schema.org Actions:** [ReserveAction](https://schema.org/ReserveAction), n.d., is a real vocabulary. Describing a reservation does not itself provide inventory, authorization or executable checkout. **MCP/NLWeb** have working implementations: [Microsoft’s launch, 19 May 2025](https://news.microsoft.com/source/features/company-news/introducing-nlweb-bringing-conversational-interfaces-directly-to-the-web/) and [repository](https://github.com/nlweb-ai/NLWeb), n.d. Search-inclusion benefits from installing them: **NOT FOUND**. ([schema.org](https://schema.org/ReserveAction))
- **WebMCP:** real experimental browser APIs, including a Chrome 149 origin trial—not merely vaporware. OpenAI also documents website-provided WebMCP tools in its desktop browser. Neither establishes universal deployment. Sources: [Chrome](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/webmcp/), [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001423), n.d. ([developer.chrome.com](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/ai/webmcp/?authuser=4))
- **Commerce:** OpenAI/Stripe’s [ACP specification](https://github.com/agentic-commerce-protocol/agentic-commerce-protocol) remains beta; OpenAI expanded it into product discovery and merchant shopping experiences on [24 March 2026](https://openai.com/index/powering-product-discovery-in-chatgpt/). Google introduced [UCP, 11 January 2026](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/agentic-commerce-ai-tools-protocol-retailers-platforms/) and donated [AP2 to FIDO, 28 April 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/agent-payments-protocol-fido-alliance/). Camp-directory eligibility and operator adoption: **NOT FOUND**. ([github.com](https://github.com/agentic-commerce-protocol/agentic-commerce-protocol))
- **Crawl controls:** Cloudflare Pay Per Crawl remained [closed beta, 28 July 2026](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/pay-per-crawl/what-is-pay-per-crawl/). [Content Signals, 24 September 2025](https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-signals-policy/), express usage preferences; [IETF AIPREF](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/aipref/about/), n.d., develops vocabulary and transport standards. Universal enforcement or material publisher earnings: **NOT FOUND**. ([developers.cloudflare.com](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/features/pay-per-crawl/what-is-pay-per-crawl/?utm_source=openai))

### Five wastes of effort

1. Rebuilding the successful homepage solely for AI; static additions address the identified retrieval gap.
2. Treating llms.txt, FAQ schema or tiny content chunks as ranking switches. [Google, 10 July 2026](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). ([developers.google.com](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide?authuser=4&hl=en))

5. Buying expensive citation dashboards or manufacturing forum mentions instead of measuring qualified referrals and booking outcomes. This is a site-scale prioritization judgment, not a measured universal rule.
