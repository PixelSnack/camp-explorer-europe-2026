Bing's own caveats, recorded so they are not lost: the figures are "a sample of overall activity" and may be refined, and the intent and topic labels are AI generated.

**What this means, stated carefully.** On the trust question, which is the exact question this directory exists to answer, Copilot cites us in roughly half of the answers it grounds. The pattern repeats across German, Spanish and French. That is a far stronger position than our Google ranking alone suggests.

**The uncomfortable half of the finding.** 14,900 citations produced only 117 Bing sessions in GA4 across all of 2026. Citation is not a click. The assistant reads our verified data, answers the parent's question, and the parent never arrives. This does not contradict the AI-search research record of 13 September, which measured GA4 *referral sessions* and correctly found almost nothing from Copilot; it measures a different thing, and both are true at once.

**Why it matters for the static wave.** The assistants are already grounding on us at scale. The plan's premise, that machine readable verified facts are the asset, is now supported by direct evidence rather than inference. It also sharpens the open question: whether to optimise for citation volume or for the click, and whether a citation with no click has any value we can sell to an operator.
