## Implications for the title-tag decision

1. The year-bearing minority is real but smaller than the August read: 18%% of top-20 clicks over 90 days, 7%% over the last 28 days, and it is the segment that converts best in season (CTR 9 to 11%% January to May).
2. There is a clean window: "2027" queries do not exist yet, and last cycle the new-year queries appeared in September and earned clicks from October. Whatever the title says must be in place and re-crawled before October.
3. Position degraded from 7 to 8 (April to July) to 13 (August). Seasonal trough is the main driver, but a stale "2026" title during the 2027 ramp would add a staleness signal on top.
4. The head terms ("summer camp europe", "summer camps europe", "european summer camps") are year-less and carry 60%% or more of clicks; they are the asset to protect. The title should serve them first.
