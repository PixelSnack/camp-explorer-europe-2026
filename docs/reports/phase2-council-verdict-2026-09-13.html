01 · What changed

From a rebuild to a sidecar

The 2025 plan

React Router + static generation across the whole app

  • Real routes for guide, countries, categories and every camp
  • The 4,700-line homepage component rendered on the server and hydrated
  • A duplicate discovery URL beside the homepage
  • Gated on 1,000 monthly sessions
  • Promised 3 to 5x traffic within 60 days
The council's decision

A post-build generator that only adds files

  • Runs after the unchanged Vite build, writes dist/slug/index.html
  • Never imports the app; plain HTML, no React on the client
  • Homepage protected by a hash check on every build
  • Gated on verification, ranking risk and the season calendar
  • Funded as a bounded experiment with no promised return

The 1,000-session gate is retired. Traffic never was the constraint. Verified facts, the ranking URL and the booking window are.

04 · Why this plan

Five facts the decision rests on

87%

of clicks land on one URL

1,095 of 1,258 Search Console clicks in twelve months came to the homepage. Anything that edits it is a risk to the whole business. So nothing does.

71

country-query impressions, zero clicks

We hold none of the geographic intent today. Country pages are net-new demand, not a reshuffle of what we have. They are also unmeasured demand, which is why no forecast is a promise.

0

competitors with dated, sourced prices

World Camps has 206 European camps and charges operators USD 990 to 1,590 a year, but shows US dollars, no dates and one price in 32 Swiss rows. Verified 2027 dates with per-child prices is the moat.

4

verified operators, or no page

Thin country pages can drag the whole hostname's quality signal down. A standalone page needs four verified operators and a fact the ranking pages lack. Czech Republic with one camp gets no page.

Nov

is when 2027 booking starts

Queries for 2027 begin in October; booking runs November to February. Pages need indexing lead time, and the winter window is open now. That is why winter goes first and why nothing waits for a quiet month.

1

engineer is not the constraint

You corrected the council: build capacity is ample and parallel. The scarce resources are your review time and verified facts. The plan spends build capacity freely and your attention only on decisions.

06 · What could go wrong

The failures the seats named, and the cheap test for each

The winter page cannot rank inside its own booking window

New paths on a one-URL hostname can take six to sixteen weeks to settle. Shipped 26 September for demand peaking October to December, it may arrive as the window closes. Two seats named this as the decision most likely to be wrong.

Test: days to indexing and to first impression of the how-we-verify page. More than ten days, and batch two reorders to query value.

Prose goes stale and nobody owns it

Today every fact has one surface, the card, generated from the data. A second surface is a second place to be wrong, and it needs no mistake, only time. World Camps' "2026 dates coming soon" in mid-September is exactly this disease.

Test: already run. Pushes reached production on 13 of the last 30 days and none for six months this spring. Hence the expiry rule and the weekly rebuild.

Valid sources joined into an invalid offer

A day-camp price, another session's age range and an operator-wide language claim, each true, combined into a programme that does not exist. The current validators check fields, not joins.

Test: a rendered-offer evidence packet per page, keyed by operator, programme, session and package, reviewed before release.

Thin country pages drag the hostname down

A cluster of pages with 85 percent boilerplate and two cards can lower the aggregate quality signal on the root that earns everything.

Test: the four-operator floor, and the fixed head-query cohort on the homepage watched weekly by device and country. Sustained deterioration pauses expansion and triggers diagnosis. Technical or consent regressions stop deployment at once.

The demand is not there

Every upside number any seat produced rests on search volumes nobody has measured. Our own history is 71 impressions.

Test: the keyword pull before batch 1a, including Astra's reconstruction: can documented visibility assumptions on the mapped queries produce the 8,000 monthly impressions its own February scenario needs? If not, that scenario is discarded before anything is built on it.

08 · What you do

Four things only you can do

1

Create the Vercel deploy hook (about three minutes)

The weekly rebuild workflow is already committed and does nothing until this secret exists.

  1. Open vercel.com, pick the European Summer Camps project, then Settings in the project's top bar.
  2. In the left menu choose Git, scroll to Deploy Hooks.
  3. Name it weekly-rebuild, branch main, click Create Hook, then Copy the URL it shows. Treat that URL like a password: anyone holding it can start builds.
  4. Open the GitHub repository in a browser, Settings tab, then Secrets and variables, then Actions, then New repository secret.
  5. Name: VERCEL_DEPLOY_HOOK_URL. Value: paste the URL. Save.
  6. Optional check: GitHub Actions tab, workflow "Weekly rebuild", Run workflow. A green run means Vercel answered; a new deployment appears in Vercel a minute later.

09 · AI answer engines

Staying attractive to ChatGPT and Gemini

ChatGPT brings roughly a quarter of organic sessions. The two engines reach us by different roads, and the plan serves both without touching the homepage.

EngineHow it reads us todayWhat the plan adds
ChatGPTTwo roads. Its search answers draw on Bing's index, where we rank second and are cited. Its own fetchers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User) are all allowed in robots.txt, but they do not run JavaScript, so on the homepage they see only the title, the description and the structured data: no camp names, no prices, no dates.Every wave-one page is plain HTML: the winter page, the how-we-verify page and the country pages give these fetchers verified 2027 dates and per-child prices to quote, with a page to cite as the source of the verification claim.
Gemini and AI OverviewsGoogle's own crawler, which renders the full grid (your crawled-page copy proves it). One flag: robots.txt disallows Google-Extended, the token Google uses for Gemini training and grounding. Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode use the ordinary crawler and are unaffected; the Gemini app's grounding may be. This was a deliberate anti-scraping choice in 2025.Your decision: keep Google-Extended blocked (no training on our verified data) or allow it (more Gemini citations, at the cost of feeding training). The lead recommends allowing it: the data is public on the page anyway, and a citation is worth more than the principle.
Perplexity, ClaudeAllowed (PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, ClaudeBot). Same JavaScript limit on the homepage.Same benefit from the static pages.
Common CrawlBlocked (CCBot). Feeds many training sets, sends no visitors.Stays blocked.

Three cheap moves on top of the plan. First, structured data on every static page (ItemList and BreadcrumbList, no review schema until provenance exists), because fetchers read it first. Second, an llms.txt at the root naming the verification page, the winter page and the country pages with one line each; Astra called it optional hygiene, and it costs an hour. Third, keep the "verified on the operator's page, date shown" line visible on every card and page, since answer engines prefer sources that state their provenance. None of these edits the homepage.

What stays untouchable through all of it

The homepage's title, H1, canonical URL and booking behaviour. The 79 / 199 / 299 pricing you are mailing operators on this week. The rule that every fact on a page was read on the operator's own page. And the rule that agents and external models are adjudicated, never relayed.
