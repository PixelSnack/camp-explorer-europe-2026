## 1. Les Elfes International Winter Camp, Verbier: STRONG, 10/10

Source pages: leselfes.com/winter-camp/ (programme), leselfes.com/dates-and-pricing/ (sessions and prices), leselfes.com/winter-camp/enrol/ (enrolment form).

- Ages 6 to 17; stays of 1 to 4 weeks; season mid-December to end of April. Sessions read on the dates page: 12 Dec 2026 to 24 Apr 2027, weekly, CHF 2,200 (17 Apr week) to 2,950 (Christmas and mid-February weeks); most weeks CHF 2,750.
- Included, per the programme page: accommodation, 4 meals a day, ski or snowboard programme, 4 Vallées ski pass, supervision, après-ski activities, weekly excursions. Extras: ski equipment rental, language courses (Verbier Language School, max 7 per class), Geneva transfer, photo package.
- Accommodation: two purpose-built chalets, 70 beds each, 4-bed rooms with ensuite bathrooms, separated by gender. Supervision: 1:7 on the slopes, 24/7, on-site nurse, night guard. Founded 1987, family-run.
- Enrolment: the winter enrolment form lists the 2026-27 sessions as selectable with CHF prices, so `bookingStatus: "open"` is verified.
- Five points: 1 PASS (own chalets), 2 PASS (family-run camp), 3 PASS (per-child weekly price, inclusions listed, transfer as an extra), 4 PASS (individual bookings, overnight supervision), 5 PASS (Les Elfes staff run the camp). Winter points 5 of 5.
- Listing row: `From CHF 2,200/1 week`, `Dec 12, 2026 - Apr 24, 2027`, badge open.

Ask admissions: what does the CHF 4,400 boarder week include (lift pass, equipment hire, meals, transfers)?
- Five points: 1 PASS (boarders on campus), 2 PASS (boarding school), 3 UNCLEAR (per-child price published; inclusions not stated), 4 PASS (weekly sessions, individual registration form, boarders on campus all week), 5 PASS. Winter points 4 of 5 (dates, price, English, established 1947; pass and instruction inclusion not stated).
- Preview row corrected: the "ski pass and equipment included" highlight was removed; listing figures otherwise unchanged. Becomes STRONG when admissions confirm the inclusions.

Source page: hif.ch/camps/winter-camp-2027/ (read in Chrome; the site refuses curl's TLS handshake).

- Ages 10 to 16; English; 9 to 19 February 2027; maximum 30 students; programme fee CHF 4,586 for 11 days and 10 nights, residential at HIF. Optional extras: Zurich Airport pick-up CHF 300, single room CHF 500 or CHF 1,000 with ensuite.
