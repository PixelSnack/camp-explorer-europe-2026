Every published figure was read by me on the operator's own page or PDF on 10 September 2026; nothing was taken from a research agent unchecked.*

**Promised to Les Elfes for Monday 14 September. Live on the 10th, four days early.**

---

## The headline, stated plainly

The owner asked for 15 winter camps at opening. **I published 6.** I am not going to dress that up.

The reason is not effort. Seven research agents swept France, the Alpine DACH countries and Italy, Poland with Czechia and Slovakia, Sweden with Finland and Denmark, Norway alone, the Baltics, and Spain with Bulgaria, Romania and Scotland. What came back is that in early September most European winter operators have not yet published next-season figures, and a large share of those that have are either members-only, school-groups-only, or sell a price that exists only with coach travel bundled in. Those all fail the five-point test on purpose.

The choice was therefore between publishing six camps whose every number I had read myself, or padding to fifteen with figures I could not stand behind on a live site that families use to make booking decisions. On a directory whose entire proposition is verified accuracy, padding is the one option that actually damages the business.

Six is above the publish gate of four, and the section grows from here as operators publish.

### Why each one earned its place

**Les Elfes (73).** Ten out of ten, and our paying Premium partner. Read tonight on their own dates page: the winter block runs weekly from 12 December 2026 to 24 April 2027 at CHF 2,200 to 2,950, and the winter page's own summary line, "Starting from CHF 2'200 per week", matches the session table exactly. Every session carries a live ENROL NOW, which is what verifies the "Booking open" badge; a marketing page announces, a form sells. Card image is their own photograph, which they chose over an illustration.

**FILOLO (74).** The value entry at CHF 999 a week with accommodation, full board and the ski pass included, across three linked ski areas. A "Bring a Friend" rate of CHF 949 exists; I listed 999 because that is what a first-time family actually pays. No founding year is printed anywhere on their site, so `established` is null rather than guessed.

**Ecole d'Humanite (75).** The most distinctive listing in the set: two weeks inside a working boarding school in the middle of its school year, twelve places, lift passes and equipment included. **I corrected an error here.** The 6 September vetting recorded ages 12 to 16; the operator's page tonight says 13 to 16. My reading wins and the row says 13 to 16.

**La Garenne (76).** An international school since 1947, five full ski days a week, eight weekly sessions. Two things a parent must know are on the card: the residential price is the boarder rate of CHF 4,400 (the CHF 3,750 figure is day-student and not residential, so it would have been misleading as a headline), and Super Ski requires an intermediate level, meaning red runs. Their own page mistypes the year on two of eight sessions as 2026; the sequence is unambiguous and the provenance comment records it.

**Prefleuri (77).** Verified from their own 2027 tariff PDF, not from the website, because the site's winter-fees page is still showing 2026 figures. This is exactly the trap the six-step search order exists for. Ages start at 3, the youngest in the directory. Ski lifts, skis, boots, suit and helmet are all in the price.

**AR-Sport (78).** The one that breaks the all-Swiss problem, and the only budget option. Six sessions, every one marked "wolne miejsca" above a live sign-up form, which is what verifies its badge. The five-day Kotelnica lift pass is in the price and coach travel is a separate PLN 270, so it passes point 3 cleanly. One nuance I put in the provenance comment because it costs a family real money: **skip the coach and you owe a compulsory PLN 150 slope-transfer fee.** Booking and the site are Polish only, which the card states through the languages field.

## Norway, since the owner asked for it specifically

The owner said he expects Norway to have great winter camps, and he is right that it should. A dedicated Norway agent ran, and I checked the best candidate myself. **Nothing qualified tonight**, and the pattern is consistent rather than accidental:

- The Christian youth organisations that run most Norwegian residential winter camps (KRIK, NLM, Acta/Normisjon, Misjon Sarepta) publish dates far ahead of prices; several pages still carry the 2026 edition in September 2026.
- Where a price does exist it is frequently a **members price beside a non-members price**, and membership bundling is a five-point risk we check for.
- Booking is Norwegian-only across the board.
- The strong facility-owning operators tend to run summer camps and rent the winter weeks out.

This is a "come back in October and November" answer, not a "Norway has nothing" answer. Norwegian operators publish winter prices late. It is the single highest-value follow-up in this file and it should be re-run in four to six weeks, ideally with an email to KRIK asking for the 2027 price directly.

## Tracking, checked rather than assumed

The owner's point stands: without tracking we are blind, and the referral data is what we actually sell. So this was verified by stubbing `gtag` and `window.open` over the DevTools protocol and clicking real buttons, not by reading the code and hoping.

**A gap was found and closed.** The GA4 event already carried `camp_season`, but the outbound UTM did not, so Les Elfes, who now hold both a summer and a winter card, could not have split those two referral streams in their own analytics. `utm_term` now carries the season.

**The existing parameters were not touched**, because Les Elfes' historical attribution runs through them. Captured from a real click on the summer card after the change:

```
utm_source=europeansummercamps  utm_medium=directory
utm_campaign=featured           utm_content=les-elfes-international
utm_term=summer                 (new, additive)
```

All four original values are byte-identical to what they have always been. The winter card returns the same four plus `utm_content=les-elfes-international-winter-camp` and `utm_term=winter`, so the operator can split the streams two independent ways.

Events confirmed firing on the winter cards: `camp_booking_click` with `camp_season: "winter"`, `camp_id`, `camp_category`, `camp_country` and `is_featured`, and `video_click` with the season on the Premium video button. A new `winter_view` event fires once per entry into the section so teaser clicks can be read against winter traffic.
