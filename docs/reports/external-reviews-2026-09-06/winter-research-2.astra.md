### Competitor-directory structure — five observations
1. WorldCamps’ Europe/winter category is useful for operator discovery, not session verification. ([world-camps.org](https://world-camps.org/best-winter-camps-in-europe/))
2. Juvigo’s age, language, duration and accommodation filters support efficient comparison. ([juvigo.at](https://juvigo.at/feriencamps/skicamps))
3. Juvigo’s map and destination-labelled cards help geographic screening. ([juvigo.at](https://juvigo.at/feriencamps/skicamps))
4. Review indicators are useful discovery aids, but do not establish residential facility control. ([juvigo.at](https://juvigo.at/feriencamps/skicamps))
5. Starting-price cards do not, by themselves, establish the first-time, transport-free booking total. ([juvigo.at](https://juvigo.at/feriencamps/skicamps))
