## Template (English)

Subject: Your listing on European Summer Camps: 2027 dates, and a change to how we work

Dear [name or team at CAMP],

We list CAMP on European Summer Camps (www.europeansummercamps .com), a directory of verified residential camps that families across Europe and beyond use to plan their children's summers. Your listing has been live since [month year] at no cost to you, and every family who clicks through arrives on your own booking page tagged as coming from us, so you can see the referrals in your analytics under the source "europeansummercamps".

We are now updating every listing for the 2027 season. Could you send us, or point us to, the following for 2027:

- Session dates
- The per-child price for one session, and what it includes
- Ages accepted
- The page where families should book

We check every detail against your own pages before it goes live, and we correct it as soon as you tell us.

One change from this season. Until now every camp has been listed free of charge. Each season we re-verify every listing's dates, prices and ages against the operator's own pages, and that work has grown beyond what we can absorb, so from the 2027 cycle each listing carries a base fee of EUR 79 per year. It covers the listing itself and its yearly update, nothing more. Camps that want more visibility can take a Premium listing instead: EUR 299 a year, or EUR 199 for the first year on the 2027 cycle, which gives your camp a highlighted card at the top of its category, a video button and up to four updates a year. The base fee is waived while a Premium listing runs.

Your listing stays online while you decide, and nothing changes on the site today.

If you have any questions, reply to this email and I will answer personally.

Kind regards,

partnerships@europeansummercamps .com
