# Outreach template, rewritten 15 September 2026 from the owner's sent Kalkalpen mail

*The Rafa Nadal Academy reply of 15 September forced a reframe (see `2026-09-15-outreach-reframe-PROPOSAL.md` for the diagnosis). The owner edited the reframed draft himself and sent it to Nationalpark Kalkalpen at 21:29 on 15 September from partnerships@. That sent mail is the template of record below. The earlier template, `2026-09-06-wave2-data-request-and-base-fee-DRAFT.md`, is superseded for first contact.*

## The structural change

First contact asks ONE question: would you like to stay listed for 2027. It names the prices so there is no bait, but it does not request a VAT number, does not request an invoicing address, and does not say an invoice will follow. Those move to a second mail sent only after a yes. It also offers removal explicitly. This is what stops a first contact reading as a bill for an unordered service.

## What the owner changed, and the rule behind each change

Recorded as rules, not as wording, so they apply to everything outward.

| His edit | The rule |
|---|---|
| "how you come to be hearing from us" to "why you are hearing from us" | Plain over literary. Never make a reader parse a construction. |
| "added the camp ourselves" to "added the camp ourselves **to our European camp directory**" | Say what happened and to what. Do not assume the reader holds the context. |
| "We research each camp" to "We research each camp **rigorously**" | Make our labour visible. The work is the product. |
| "the operator's own pages" to "the **camp** operator's own pages" | Name who, every time. |
| "Since March" to "Since March **alone**" | Frame a figure so its partial window shows. The number is bigger than it looks. |
| "since we launched" to "since we launched **in early 2025, over 2 seasons ago**" | Give the history a concrete anchor. |
| "So from the 2027 season" to "**Due to this,** from the 2027 season" | Spell out the causal link rather than leaving it to juxtaposition. |
| "There is also a Premium option" to "**We have also decided to offer** a Premium option" | Active agency. We decide things, they do not merely exist. |
| "held at that rate" to "**locked** at that rate" | Concrete and reassuring over neutral. |
| "ahead of the standard listings in its category" plus "**for increased visibility**" | Name the benefit. Never leave the reader to infer why a feature matters. |
| "Nothing is owed for 2026" to "**For now, rest assured that** nothing is owed for 2026" | Reassure explicitly, do not merely state the fact. |
| "say so and I will set out what it involves" to "**let us know and we will** set out **what you get, and what information we will need**" | Two-sided concreteness: what they receive and what we need back. |
| "just tell me and I will take the entry down" to "just **inform me here**, and **we** will take it down" | "here" names the reply channel. |
| "We would genuinely **like**" to "We would genuinely **love**" | Warmth up. |
| "if anything is **wrong**" to "if anything on your **current** listing is **inaccurate**" | Professional register for negatives. |
| "You are welcome to answer in German" to "You are **also** welcome" | The same "also" edit he made on the GLS round, 13 September. |
| One long Premium paragraph split into three short ones | Short paragraphs. Break at every distinct idea. A one-sentence paragraph is fine. |

## The voice rule, refined

He did NOT convert everything to "we". The split he actually applied:

- **"I" for the founder's own acts:** "I am writing", "I will follow up separately", "I will answer promptly", "inform me here".
- **"we" for the company's acts and commitments:** "we will set out", "we will take the entry down", "we are asking", "we have also decided to offer", "we would genuinely love".

The founder speaks as I; the business acts as we. This supersedes the flat "always we, never I" note in the older template.

## One word changed back

He wrote "We research each camp **vigorously**". The table above says **rigorously**, and that is what the remaining drafts use. Vigorously means with force or energy; rigorously means with thoroughness and precision, which is the claim we are actually making about verification. Flagged to the owner rather than applied silently. The Kalkalpen mail is already sent with "vigorously" and is not being chased.

## One claim the owner owns

"since we launched in early 2025, over 2 seasons ago". The repository's first commit is 23 August 2025, so our own records show a later launch than "early 2025". The owner asked for this twice and wrote it himself into a sent mail, so it stands and the remaining drafts carry it. Noted here so the discrepancy is not lost, and because the Nadal reply deliberately does not repeat it: that correspondent has already challenged us once over an overstatement.

## Standing mechanics

- Web addresses written with a space before the top-level domain; the owner closes it and the dot must stay.
- No links, no em dash, no internal comment or marker anywhere in a mail that will be sent, subject included.
- Referral figures carried across verbatim, never recalculated. A figure only at 20 or more.
- Two-camp operators get one mail covering both, with the combined figure.
- Sender set to partnerships@ before sending. The From row in the Gmail composer only appears once the recipient field has focus.
