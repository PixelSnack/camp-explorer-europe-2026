## The thinking behind the framing

- **Lead with what they got, in their own terms.** Camps judge a directory by referrals, not by badges. The one number that matters to them is families sent to their site. Give it plainly, with the caveat that per-camp counting only began in March, so the true figure since January is higher. Point them to their own analytics, where we appear as the source "europeansummercamps", so the claim is checkable without trusting us.
- **The extension is a decision already taken, not a negotiation.** "We have extended" reads as generosity with a clear end date; "we could extend" invites haggling. The end date (31 December 2026) also sets up the 2027 cycle without a second email.
- **Winter is news, and it flatters them accurately.** They are the first camp in the section because their winter programme was the only one that passed every check on the operator's own pages. Say that, briefly; it is true and it explains why they are there.
- **The 2027 change in one paragraph, no apology.** State the base fee and Premium as facts of the new cycle, with the reason (seasonal re-verification), and make the ask small: confirm which option they want by a date. Nothing about "increased administration costs" in the abstract; name the work instead.
- **Two listings, one decision.** The policy says a Premium subscription covers one listing and bundles are the owner's call. The draft carries the owner's choice in brackets; the recommendation is Option B, because they are the launch camp of the winter section and one invoice is simpler for both sides.
- **No promises beyond the listing.** No traffic forecasts, no ranking claims, no "exposure". The referral figure is history, not a prediction.
- **Send timing.** The paragraph on winter says "now live". Send on the day the winter section publishes; before that, change it to "goes live on [date]".
