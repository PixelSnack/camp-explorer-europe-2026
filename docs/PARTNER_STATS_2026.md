# European Summer Camps: Audience and Performance Snapshot

*Partner-facing figures. Every number below comes from Google Analytics 4 for europeansummercamps.com and can be shared with camps, in emails and in listing conversations. Updated 17 August 2026. Figures count consenting visitors only, so real reach is higher.*

## The audience in one paragraph

European Summer Camps is a curated directory of 65 verified camp organisations across 24 European countries, used by parents who are actively choosing a camp for their children. Visitors arrive overwhelmingly through search engines and AI assistants, stay engaged at a rate few directories reach, and one in three clicks straight through to a camp website. The site ranks on the first page of Google and Bing for its core queries and has grown every season since launch without paid advertising.

**82% of visitors arrive with a question already formed.** They typed "summer camp in Switzerland for a 12 year old" into Google or asked ChatGPT for recommendations, and European Summer Camps is what they were shown. ChatGPT alone is now the second largest source of traffic, ahead of Bing by four to one, because the site is built to be read by AI assistants as well as by people.
